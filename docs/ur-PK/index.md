The Appearance card has two independent switches. **Animations** runs `argvus effects-toggle animations toggle` and controls everything that moves. **Effects** runs `argvus effects-toggle effects toggle` and switches blur and the transparency of Terminal, Launcher, Widget Telemetry, Control Panel and Taskbar together; it shows as on when at least one of them is on. **SUPER + F6** (`argvus effects-toggle toggle`) switches both together. Changing either reloads Hyprland and the theme consumers in place: Waybar re-reads its stylesheets, the panel reloads over IPC and Dunst reloads its configuration unless Do Not Disturb is active, in which case its reload is deferred until DND is turned off. ۔ﯽﺗﻮﮨ ﮟﯿﮩﻧ ﺮﮨﺎﻇ ﻦﯾﺮﮑﺳ ﮓﻧﮈﻮﻟ ﯽﺋﻮﮐ ﺭﻭﺍ ﺎﺗﻮﮨ ﮟﯿﮩﻧ ﻉﻭﺮﺷ ﮦﺭﺎﺑﻭﺩ ﯽﮭ
